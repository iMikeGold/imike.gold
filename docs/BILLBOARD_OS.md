# BillBoard OS

## Role

BillBoard OS is the threshold interface at `imike.gold`.

It is not a portfolio, biography, archive, directory, or content publication.
It is a one-screen selection surface that directs a visitor into one of three
different encounters with the wider Mike Gold system.

## Entry points

### Enter the System

- Destination: `imikegold.com`
- World: Memory OS, archive, music, fragments, recovered material
- Media direction: real interface capture; documentary rather than synthetic

### Explore the Works

- Destination: `houseofgold.online`
- World: designed objects, brands, publications, environments and connected works
- Media direction: constructed or impossible House of Gold environment

### Meet the Manchild

- Destination: `mikegold.co.uk`
- World: engineering practice, systems, tools and the maker behind them
- Media direction: hybrid real footage; technical evidence interrupted by play

## Governing rules

1. Exactly one viewport.
2. No page navigation.
3. No footer.
4. No project grid.
5. No conventional case-study listing.
6. At rest, the worlds are withheld.
7. Interaction introduces colour, movement and evidence.
8. The selected world expands; the others recede.
9. Media is atmosphere, not explanation.
10. The gateway remains functional while every media bay is empty.

## Power states

### Standby

The structural billboard is live, but no destination video is connected.
Procedural standby graphics occupy each distribution-feed bay.

### Distribution feed loaded

A configured video remains paused until its panel is hovered, focused or selected. The surrounding interaction,
copy and destination link remain unchanged.

## Content boundary

All destination definitions belong in `main-site/src/config/gateway.ts` when the project uses a `src` directory, or `main-site/config/gateway.ts` otherwise.
The billboard should not gain a CMS, Markdown archive, date system, search
engine or general-purpose content layer unless its role changes fundamentally.
